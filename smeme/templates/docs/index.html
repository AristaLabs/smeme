Why SMEme

Business rules don’t belong in prompts. A prompt can describe your procedure, but the model still decides each time how to apply it. Ask twice and it may weigh things differently, skip a step, or fill a gap with a plausible guess. That’s fine for a first draft. It isn’t fine for a decision you have to make the same way every time and explain afterward.

SMEme is rational AI. It reasons from your rules to the conclusions they force, using logic instead of predicting what sounds right. Your procedure lives in a decision tree you can read and approve. Your chatbot does what it’s good at: finding the facts in your files and apps. SMEme applies your procedure to those facts and works out what follows.

  • Same facts, same outcome. Run a case today or next month and you get the same result.
  • Your procedure, not the model’s opinion. Every outcome traces to a rule you approved, and you can open the tree and read it.
  • It asks only what still matters. After each answer, SMEme works out which open questions can still change the outcome and asks only for those. Your agent doesn’t spend time on evidence that wouldn’t make a difference.
  • It won’t guess. If the answers don’t settle the case, contradict each other, or come from sources that disagree, the report says so instead of picking one.
  • It shows its work. The report lists the path, the evidence behind each answer, and which answers decided the outcome.
  • You can ask before you act. Find out what would change the outcome, or what it would take to reach a different one.
  • Changes are checked. Your past cases run every time you Deploy, so an edit can’t quietly change a decision you’ve already made.
