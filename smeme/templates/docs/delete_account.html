Delete your account

SMEme account closure is self-service from your profile. It permanently removes your account and all data tied to it. This is separate from deleting an individual decision tree or managing sign-in in Clerk.

Where to delete

Open Profile and scroll to the Danger zone card at the bottom. Click Delete account permanently.

Account deletion is managed in SMEme — not in Clerk. Clerk handles email, password, and sign-in only. Deleting your account in the Clerk Account Portal is disabled so you always see SMEme's warning first.

Confirmation steps

Deletion uses the same two-step pattern as permanent decision tree removal:

  1. Review — a modal lists everything that will be removed, including in-progress wizard generations and an active Pro subscription.
  2. Confirm — type delete my account permanently (case and surrounding spaces are ignored) and submit.

After success you are signed out and see a confirmation page. You cannot undo this action.

What is removed

Account deletion hard-deletes your SMEme user record and owned data from active systems, including:

  • All decision trees and every version in each family
  • Deployed decision trees and AI evaluation history
  • Sessions, memos, and creator profile data
  • In-progress agentic generations (canceled immediately; not saved)
  • Your Clerk identity (sign-in for this account)

If you have an active Pro subscription, it is canceled immediately as part of deletion — including any time you already paid for in the current billing period.

Pro subscription & re-signup

Deleting your SMEme account is not the same as canceling Pro in the Stripe Customer Portal. Portal cancellation normally keeps Pro access until the end of the paid period. Account deletion ends Pro access immediately and forfeits any remaining prepaid time. We do not prorate or refund unused days when you delete your account.

If you sign up again with the same email, you get a new SMEme account. We do not reconnect you to a previous Stripe subscription or restore forfeited time. Upgrading again starts a new subscription and new billing period. To keep Pro through a period you already paid for, cancel via the Stripe portal from Profile instead of deleting your account.

What may be retained

  • Payment and billing records held by Stripe as required for tax and accounting
  • Short-lived database backup copies until your provider's rotation window expires
  • Redacted audit events (event types preserved; personal identifiers removed)

See our Privacy Policy for retention and deletion rights.

Not the same as deleting a decision tree

Removing one decision tree from the dashboard frees a decision tree slot but keeps your account, other decision trees, and billing. Confirm phrase: delete decision tree permanently. See Deploy, list, & delete for decision tree removal.

To keep a copy of a graph before closing your account, use Download your decision tree. Export is per decision tree and does not include deployed artifacts or session history.
